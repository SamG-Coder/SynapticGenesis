# Broader replay improves the completed prose learners

Increasing the ordinary replay reservoir from 1,024 to 16,384 descriptors
improved the final validation mean from **1.8000325 to 1.688728 nats per byte**
for the 2M model and from **1.967449 to 1.668709** for the 105M model. Every
final validation book and both early-reader retention books improved at each
size. The generated question answers remain unsuccessful.

This is one exploratory seed using the [declared protocol](prose-replay-capacity.md).
The [complete result](../reports/prose-replay-capacity-results.json) preserves
all eight endpoints, 48 book scores, 32 raw generations and both learning
sessions. The [publication audit](../reports/prose-replay-capacity-audit.json)
verifies all 82 candidate native commands and 80 corresponding baseline
assessment commands against their saved arguments and outputs.

## Matched exposure and actual differences

Both fresh candidates began with exactly the same parameter, Adam-moment,
recurrent-state and hyperparameter arrays as their corresponding controls.
Only initial replay capacity and the resulting checkpoint checksum differ.
They use the same native executable, 55 selected training books, source order,
learning rate, byte vocabulary, architecture and seed. No learned parent or
external teacher was imported.

Each model completed 216,289 source observations, 27,680,129 source target
pairs, 54,072 replay updates and 41,472 generated bytes. The larger reservoirs
changed the selected replay windows and consumed **6,916,094 replay target
pairs**, compared with **6,918,179** for the original controls: 2,085 fewer.
Equal update counts do not mean identical replay examples or per-stage counts.
The two candidate sizes have identical replay-policy payloads at all four
endpoints, so they received the same candidate replay history.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2M | 1 | 2.200162 | 2.199028 | -0.001134 |
| 2M | 2 | 1.911111 | 1.884107 | -0.027004 |
| 2M | 3 | 1.769407 | 1.720090 | -0.049317 |
| 2M | 4 | 1.800032 | 1.688728 | -0.111304 |
| 105M | 1 | 2.192909 | 2.180658 | -0.012250 |
| 105M | 2 | 1.866186 | 1.825902 | -0.040285 |
| 105M | 3 | 1.804779 | 1.703869 | -0.100910 |
| 105M | 4 | 1.967449 | 1.668709 | -0.298740 |

These are equal-book means over the same four 65,536-byte validation samples;
lower is better. The first-stage per-book changes are mixed despite the small
mean improvement. At the final endpoint, the larger-pool 105M model is also
slightly better than the larger-pool 2M model on all six monitored books. That
does not establish an optimized size ranking or a general scaling law.

## What the learner rehearsed

The [new coverage reconstruction](../reports/prose-replay-capacity-coverage.json)
replays the host policy from the actual stage-three state. All 30,271
final-stage replay events reproduce the native stage-four replay payload,
source cursor, counters and random-number states exactly. The original
1,024-slot coverage rows also reproduce unchanged under the extended audit.

| Source stage revisited during stage four | Distinct windows, 1,024 slots | Distinct windows, 16,384 slots | Larger-pool replay updates |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1: foundations | 243 | 1,807 | 7,603 |
| 2 | 256 | 3,489 | 7,617 |
| 3 | 256 | 3,432 | 7,517 |
| 4: current material | 1,045 | 5,070 | 7,534 |

Across the three earlier stages, coverage rises from **755 to 8,728 distinct
windows**, or from **0.85% to 9.80%** of their 89,075 available source windows.
That is approximately 11.6 times broader coverage. Earlier-stage replay
updates are 22,737, versus 22,751 originally; total final-stage replay updates
remain 30,271. The difference is broader selection, not more optimizer steps.

Distinctness counts document/offset/length descriptors, not unique knowledge
or semantic facts. The current-stage reservoir changes over time, so windows
replayed during the interval can exceed the slots left at the end. Foundation
passes can create multiple stored descriptors for the same text position.
The reconstruction verifies host policy, not the model's biological dynamics
or an independent forward pass.

## Timing and remaining language limitations

| Completed candidate | Native live-loop seconds | Source target pairs per second |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2M | 769.16 | 35,987 |
| 105M | 3,907.78 | 7,083 |

These reported intervals include source learning, replay, scheduled speech
and in-loop checkpoint writes. Setup and the subsequent assessments are
outside those intervals. They are observations of the completed runs, not a
controlled speed improvement over the original controls, which used different
checkpoint-save frequencies for 105M. Serialized replay state is 393,512
bytes at completion; this is not a GPU memory measurement.

The final 105M output still begins as follows for two unchanged monitor
prompts. These are exact excerpts of the saved generations:

```text
Question: What is water?
Answer: Athos had never fland of woman is my pardon, and
only waiting hard behold all the sight of a cabin.
```

```text
Question: What is two plus three?
Answer: indeed the anger Catherine in this friends. She embramss had before
```

Neither excerpt answers its question. Improved next-byte prediction on the
selected prose does not establish instruction following, factual recall,
arithmetic, broad reasoning or useful general dialogue. The astronomy,
arithmetic and physics review work was not used by this experiment.

The result supports broader rehearsal as a candidate for subsequent learning
studies. It supplies no independent seed replication, test-set score or proof
that every additional replay slot is useful. No model or policy is
automatically promoted for teaching, reproduction or a default role.

## Verification and reproduction

The [rejection tests](../reports/prose-replay-capacity-verification.json)
reject fourteen altered scores, roles, raw-sample records, native arguments,
means or exposure comparisons. The [compatibility record](../reports/prose-replay-capacity-compatibility.json)
also preserves the original command policies, assessment extraction and
baseline coverage. All verification here is CPU-only and leaves the native
study outputs unchanged.

```powershell
python -X utf8 scripts/publish_replay_capacity.py --report runs/replay-results-local.json --audit runs/replay-audit-local.json
python -X utf8 scripts/prose_replay_coverage.py --experiment runs/prose-replay-capacity --profiles 2m 105m --out runs/replay-coverage-local.json
python -X utf8 tests/replay_capacity_publication.py --out runs/replay-rejections-local.json
```

Use fresh output paths. These commands verify preserved local artifacts;
they do not rerun or replace the completed learners.
