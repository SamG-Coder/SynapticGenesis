# Completed late-stage learning-rate comparison

Reducing the 105M learner's final-stage rate from 0.0003 to 0.000075 worsened
all eight book monitors at both measured endpoints. The candidate's final
four-book validation mean was **2.325731 nats/byte**, compared with **1.967449**
for the original-rate control and **1.804779** for their shared parent. This
experiment does not support adopting that late rate reduction.

The [complete native result](../reports/prose-retention-rates.json) and
[publication audit](../reports/prose-retention-rates-audit.json) retain all
scores, twenty raw generations, four learning sessions and endpoint state.
All 64 native commands completed successfully. The resumed original-rate
control is byte-identical to the original completed checkpoint, and both
arms have identical source/replay/speech counters, complete replay payloads,
source cursors and random-number state at their matched endpoints.

## Matched learning and measurements

Both arms inherit the same 104,851,472-parameter, 32,768-neuron checkpoint
after 95,207 source observations. Each receives the same remaining 15,497,256
source next-byte targets through the final 21 training books, plus identical
replay and scheduled-generation exposure. Learning and generation use the
existing shared native C++/CUDA model. Optimizer moments and recurrent state
survive each restart. Only the declared learning rate changes between arms.

Book scores use the original strict-FP32 assessment: 65,536 sampled target
bytes per book through independent 128-byte windows. The validation mean
weights four books equally. Lower loss is better. Training-book monitors
measure fit to old or new training material and are not held-out scores.

Halfway is 155,748 source observations; final is 216,289. The lower rate's
validation mean is 0.154267 worse than the control halfway and 0.358281 worse
at completion. Its final mean is 0.520952 worse than the parent, compared
with the control's 0.162670 regression.

The last new-stage book improves relative to the parent under both rates,
but the quarter-rate arm improves less. Its other seven monitors finish
worse than their parent values. There is no observed retention benefit to
trade against its weaker new-book fit in this run.

## Raw final question probes

These excerpts preserve the candidate's spelling and punctuation. Complete
outputs and byte hashes are retained in the report. The same four prompts,
seed 42, temperature 0.8, top-k 40 and 256 generated byte tokens are used at
every assessment, with reset recurrence and graph generation in strict FP32.

```text
Question: What is water?
Answer: "Pty I should like to be one another more
friend; she relieutenant livited away."
```

```text
Question: What is two plus three?
Answer: the baron is an escape in this woman armed and
came forward in it.
```

Both fail to answer the questions. These are qualitative probes, not a
statistical QA benchmark. Nothing in this experiment demonstrates useful
general question answering.
