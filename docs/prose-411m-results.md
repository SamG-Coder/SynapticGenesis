# Completed 411M prose comparison

The 411,028,496-parameter model completed the selected 55-book curriculum, but
its final validation loss is **1.724522 nats per byte**, versus **1.668709** for
the 104,851,472-parameter control. Every monitored book is worse at the final
endpoint, and the larger model trails at all four stages. Its fixed question
completions still fail to answer the questions. This run establishes full
learning, persistence and assessment at the larger size, not a quality gain.

The [complete result](../reports/prose-411m-results.json) preserves every stage,
all 48 book scores and 32 raw generations across the two sizes. The
[publication audit](../reports/prose-411m-audit.json) independently verifies
one learning command and 80 saved assessment commands, checkpoint identities,
source/replay/speech exposure, original baseline scores and all generated bytes.
The [declared protocol](prose-411m.md) retains the preflight and original launch.

## Capacity and matched exposure

| Model | Parameters | Channels | Neurons per layer | Layers | Total spiking neurons |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 105M control | 104,851,472 | 1,024 | 4,096 | 8 | 32,768 |
| 411M candidate | 411,028,496 | 2,048 | 8,192 | 8 | 65,536 |

The candidate has 3.92 times the parameters and twice the spiking state units.
These counts are specific to this architecture; they are not biological neuron
equivalents. Both founders began from randomized weights with seed 1337.
There are no imported pretrained, teacher or parent weights.

The source edition contains 4,817,047 word occurrences and 26,896,416 UTF-8
bytes. Each model completed 216,289 source observations, 27,680,129 source
next-byte targets, 54,072 replay updates with 6,916,094 replay targets, and
41,472 generated bytes. Generation uses the live model but does not supply
training targets. Both have exactly matching source cursors, replay payloads,
replay random states and speech policy at every assessed endpoint.

The extra source exposure above the unique edition size comes from the
declared foundation passes; it is not additional authored material. The
astronomy, arithmetic and Physics preparations are not used in this study.

## Full-run speed and activity

| Completed learner | Native live-loop seconds | Source byte targets per second |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 105M | 3,907.78 | 7,083.34 |
| 411M | 9,729.39 | 2,845.00 |

The 411M loop took about 2 hours 42 minutes. These intervals include learning,
replay, scheduled generation, logging and in-loop checkpoint writes. They
exclude process/model setup and the subsequent book and sample assessments.
The control saves periodically every 2,048 observations; the candidate saves
every 8,192. They also use their declared preserved executables. These are
observed session costs, not an isolated timing test of model width.

The separate [short capacity benchmark](411m-capacity-results.md) measured
1,340.47 generated bytes/s at 105M and 456.40 at 411M after warm-up. Its source
fixture, replay capacity and exposure differ from this full run. Those decode
rates and its 8.464 GiB of counted 411M live arrays must not be presented as
new full-run generation or continuous peak-memory measurements.

All tokens here are UTF-8 bytes, not subword tokens. In the final logged
411M training interval, about 98.25% of the measured neuron-time positions
emit a signed spike. High activity remains a concern, but it does not by
itself explain the quality regression or establish the benefit of a penalty.
The [controlled membrane-objective study](membrane-learning.md) tests that
separate hypothesis using same-seed controls and language/retention scores.

## Verification and interpretation

`scripts/publish_large_founder.py` runs the frozen
`large_founder_verification.py` in a fresh Python interpreter. This avoids
mixing its checkpoint reader with later readers from the main checkout.
The audit records hashes of the verifier's imported project modules and
authenticates the declared inputs, saved commands, checkpoint metadata and
raw outputs. The public result is byte-identical to the completed study.
Publication launches no CUDA command and adds no learning updates.

From the main workspace, with the preserved local study and verifier checkout:

```powershell
python -X utf8 scripts/publish_large_founder.py --protocol-sha256 daba74f6fecd44f8fd9334c07d07120bd5b9ac4466f46e9e2fa741f38c851244 --report runs/411m-result-local.json --audit runs/411m-audit-local.json
```

Use fresh publication paths. Incomplete or failed studies and colliding output
paths are refused. This is evidence verification, not another independent
neural numerical oracle. It adds no test-set score or seed replication.

One seed, an unchanged exposure budget and an untuned common learning rate do
not establish an optimal model size or general scaling law. More capacity did
not improve this run. Further expansion should follow demonstrated learning
and retention gains; no founder is promoted for teaching or reproduction by
these results.
